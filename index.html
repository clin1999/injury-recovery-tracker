How to export from Garmin Connect
  1. Sign in at connect.garmin.com, ideally on a computer.
  2. Go to Activities, then All activities.
  3. Scroll down until the list reaches your programme start date, so older runs are included.
  4. Select Export CSV at the top of the list.
  5. Save the file somewhere your phone can open it, such as iCloud Drive or Google Drive, then choose it here.

The file is read on this device and isn’t uploaded anywhere. Distances and dates come across. Pain scores don’t, so add those yourself.
